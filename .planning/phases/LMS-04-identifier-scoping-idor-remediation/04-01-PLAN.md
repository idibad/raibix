<objective>
Remediate Insecure Direct Object References (IDOR) across fee challans, payments, and checkout flows by enforcing object-level authorization policies and relationship scoping.

Purpose: Prevent students and parents from viewing, downloading, paying, or uploading receipts for fee challans belonging to other students.
Output: Scoped controllers and automated feature test suite verifying IDOR prevention on fee endpoints.
</objective>

    app/Http/Controllers/Tenant/ParentPortalController.php,
    app/Http/Controllers/Tenant/PaymentController.php
  </read_first>
  <action>
    1. In `FeeStructureController::printChallan($id)`:
       - Load `$challan = FeeChallan::with(['student.currentClass', 'transactions'])->findOrFail($id);`
       - Enforce `Gate::authorize('view', $challan);`
    2. In `ParentPortalController::payChallan(int $challanId, ...)`:
       - Load `$challan = FeeChallan::findOrFail($challanId);`
       - Enforce `Gate::authorize('pay', $challan);`
    3. In `PaymentController::checkout(Request $request, $id)`:
       - Load `$challan = FeeChallan::where('id', $id)->where('status', '!=', 'paid')->firstOrFail();`
       - Enforce `Gate::authorize('pay', $challan);`
  </action>
  <verify>
    php artisan test
  </verify>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - Gate::authorize('view', $challan) and Gate::authorize('pay', $challan) guard all fee print and payment actions
  </acceptance_criteria>
  <done>Controllers scoped with authorization checks.</done>
</task>
