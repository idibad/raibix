<objective>
Audit and scope student records, report cards, leaves, assignment submissions, and academic resources to prevent IDOR across portal roles.

Purpose: Prevent parents, students, and teachers from modifying or accessing records outside their immediate academic assignments and family units.
Output: Scoped academic and portal controllers with automated feature test coverage.
</objective>

<tasks>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Task 1: Scope ParentPortalController and StudentPortalController</name>
  <files>
    app/Http/Controllers/Tenant/ParentPortalController.php,
    app/Http/Controllers/Tenant/StudentPortalController.php
  </files>
  <read_first>
    app/Http/Controllers/Tenant/ParentPortalController.php,
    app/Http/Controllers/Tenant/StudentPortalController.php
  </read_first>
  <action>
    1. In `ParentPortalController::submitLeaveRequest`:
       - Verify that `$validated['student_id']` belongs to `$user->parentsProfile->students()->pluck('id')->toArray()`.
       - If not in the parent's children list, abort with 403 Forbidden.
    2. In `StudentPortalController::askQuestion` and `StudentPortalController::submitAssignment`:
       - Retrieve the student's current section and class.
       - Verify that the target assignment's section_id is null or matches the student's section/class. If not, abort with 403 Forbidden.
  </action>
  <verify>
    php artisan test
  </verify>
  <acceptance_criteria>
    - Parents cannot submit leaves for unrelated students
    - Students cannot submit work to other sections
  </acceptance_criteria>
  <done>Student and parent portal scoped.</done>
</task>
