<objective>
Implement role and permission middleware groups across `routes/tenant.php` to strictly guard Admin, Teacher, Student, and Parent portals.

Purpose: Prevent privilege escalation where any authenticated user (e.g. student or parent) could access administrative, financial, or grading endpoints.
Output: Role authorization middleware registered and applied across all tenant route groups.
</objective>
